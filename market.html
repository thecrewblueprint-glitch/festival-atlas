How the routes differ

Employer, producer, and labor organization are not the same thing.

1

Labor providers

Companies whose public business includes supplying stagehand, technical, rigging, or event labor.

2

Production technology

Lighting, audio, video, staging, scenic, broadcast, and full-service technical companies with employment routes.

3

Producers / promoters

Organizations that produce or promote events. Their presence does not prove a direct stagehand hiring route.

4

Labor organizations

IATSE locals and related mechanisms vary by jurisdiction, agreement, referral, membership, training, and employer relationship.
